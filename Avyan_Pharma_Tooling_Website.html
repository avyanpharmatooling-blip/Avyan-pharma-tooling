MATERIAL SCIENCE

Tool Steel Selection

AVYAN PHARMA TOOLING offers a series of specialty steels tailored to resolve complex tableting challenges and maximize tooling durability.

O1 Steel (OHNS)

General purpose Oil Hardened Non-Shrinking steel with high hardenability, commonly used for blanking & stamping punches.

S7 Steel (Premium Grade)

Exceptional impact resistance & highest hardenability of shock-resisting tool steels for heavy load-bearing compression punches.

D3 Steel (HCHC)

High Carbon High Chromium hot die steel with great wear resistance, making it the preferred choice for tableting dies.

D2 Steel (HCHC)

High Carbon High Chromium steel with excellent wear and abrasion resistance, ideal for abrasive tablet compression.

440C Steel

Stainless steel with high carbon and chromium (up to 60 HRC). Used for small tips in multi-tip tooling for high surface finish.

SURFACE PROTECTION

Advanced Tool Coatings

Hard Chrome Plating (HCP)

Ideal for resisting wear and corrosion in standard applications with low coefficient of friction.

  • ✓ High Hardness & Excellent Surface Finish
  • ✓ Economical for thin deposits
  • ✓ Superb abrasion resistance

Chromium Nitride (CrN) Coating

Excellent anti-stick performance, superb corrosion resistance, and biocompatibility for hygroscopic formulations.

  • ✓ Superior oxidation stability
  • ✓ Outstanding hot hardness
  • ✓ Acts as a solid film lubricant

Titanium Nitride (TiN) Coating

Gold-colored coating delivering high wear protection, extreme hardness, and low thermal conductivity.

  • ✓ Extended tooling service life
  • ✓ Excellent protection against wear & tear
  • ✓ Corrosion & heat resistance
